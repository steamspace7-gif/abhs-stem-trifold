Quick Answers

What to know before you bring a class.

What grades can attend?

Hands-on projects for 2nd–6th grade. 2nd LEGO Paper Building, 3rd Paper Circuits, 4th Laser-Cut Boxes, 5th micro:bit, 6th Puzzle Design.

How long is a typical trip?

3–4 hours on Tuesdays and Thursdays during the school year.

How many students can attend?

Projects are designed for groups of 20 or fewer. Larger classes can rotate.

Are chaperones required?

Yes. We recommend at least one adult per 10 students. Schools provide permission slips, transportation, and supervision.

What should students bring?

A signed permission slip, a water bottle, and a sack lunch if staying through lunch. We provide all project materials.

How do I schedule?

Go to steamspace.vercel.app, choose a project, then send the Field Trip Interest Form. We follow up to confirm.
